A crisis unfolding
in silence above us.

Thousands of satellites operate daily under the threat of catastrophic collision. The consequences extend far beyond a single lost satellite.

01

Tracked debris objects

Over 27,000 pieces of debris large enough to destroy a satellite are tracked in orbit — with millions more going undetected. Every launch adds to the risk.

// THE SOLUTION

Automated intelligence
for orbital safety.

CAS ingests real Space-Track conjunction data, applies physics-based risk models, and generates actionable maneuver recommendations.

CDM

Real-time Space-Track integration

Directly fetches Conjunction Data Messages from US Space Command. No manual uploads. No delays.

Pc

ESA-compliant collision probability

Foster/Chan 2D Gaussian method with J2 perturbation propagation. Same methodology as ESA Space Debris Office.

Δv

Optimal maneuver recommendations

Minimum delta-v calculations with optimal lead time. Target Pc below 0.0001% post-maneuver.

📈

Pc trend analytics

Track how collision probability evolves over time across CDM updates. Logarithmic charts with RED/YELLOW thresholds and human-readable risk display.

⚖

Source comparison

Compare Space-Track CDM data against CAS engine calculations side by side. Validate risk assessments across independent computation sources.

🔗

Cascade maneuver analysis

Full catalog screening of 10,000+ objects after each maneuver candidate. Pareto optimization balancing fuel efficiency against secondary collision safety. No blind maneuvers.

🛰

Operator watchlist

Register your satellites, get personalized conjunction alerts. Automatic hourly scanning against the full debris catalog. See only what matters to you.

ML

Historical pattern learning

Training on 2M+ historical CDM records to identify high-risk orbital regimes before they escalate.
